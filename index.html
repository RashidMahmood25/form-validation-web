Validation rules in this demo:
  • Names: at least 2 letters, letters/spaces only.
  • Email: standard format (name@domain.tld).
  • Mobile: Pakistani format — 03XX-XXXXXXX or 03XXXXXXXXX (11 digits starting with 03).
  • Date of birth: must be a real past date, age 13 or older.
  • Password: 8+ characters with a letter and a number; strength meter rewards length, mixed case, digits and symbols.
  • Confirm password must match. Terms must be accepted.
  • The submit button stays disabled until every field is valid.

Demo only — nothing is sent to a server; on success the form shows a confirmation summary.
